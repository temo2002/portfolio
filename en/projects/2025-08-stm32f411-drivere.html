hobby 2025

Bare-metal drivers for the STM32F411RE in C

Self-written peripheral drivers for the STM32F411RE microcontroller, without HAL and CMSIS. The project followed the Udemy course "Mastering Microcontroller with Embedded Driver Development".

  • C
  • STM32
  • embedded
  • I2C
  • SPI
  • USART

What it is

Register definitions and drivers for GPIO, I2C, SPI, USART and RCC, written directly against the registers in the datasheet. The drivers support both polling and interrupt driven operation, and are tried out with small example programs on a Nucleo-F411RE board.

  • GPIO with external interrupts (EXTI)
  • I2C master talking to an Arduino slave, in polling and interrupt mode
  • SPI and USART, including printf over USART2
  • Examples from blinking an LED to communication between the STM32 and an Arduino

What I take with me

  • Reading the datasheet and reference manual and turning it into working code
  • Interrupt handling and state machines in communication drivers
  • Structuring a C project with its own drivers, examples and a Makefile
