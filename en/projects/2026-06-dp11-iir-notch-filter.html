DP11 IIR notch filter

Second-order IIR notch filter that removes a 1680 Hz beep from audio sampled at 44.1 kHz, implemented in Q15 on the AVR128DB48.

  • IIR
  • notch filter
  • MATLAB
  • Q15

Result

  • Around 45 dB attenuation at the beep with minimal effect on the rest of the audio.
  • The filter needs only four state variables and five coefficients, against 165 taps in the FIR solution from DP9.
  • Verified in MATLAB before implementation in C.
