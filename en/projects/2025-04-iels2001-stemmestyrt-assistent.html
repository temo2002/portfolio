IELS2001 Voice-controlled assistant

Local voice assistant for smart homes: an ESP32 with an I2S microphone streams audio to Node-RED and Whisper, which controls lights through Home Assistant, with no cloud services.

  • ESP32
  • Whisper
  • Node-RED
  • Home Assistant
  • DSP

What we built

A group project of two in Engineering Project I. An ESP32 collects audio from a digital I2S microphone and sends it over WebSocket to Node-RED. There a WAV file is created and transcribed locally by Whisper (faster-whisper in Docker), and the text triggers automations in Home Assistant, such as switching an IKEA Trådfri bulb on and off.

Challenges and solutions

  • The original goal was a smart speaker, but ordering problems at the supplier meant the project was changed to a voice assistant without audio playback.
  • The audio crackled because of DC offset. After a DFT analysis it was solved with a digital high-pass filter.
  • MQTT was replaced with WebSocket to avoid base64 encoding and reduce latency.

Results

  • Response time of 3.2 to 5.1 seconds, against a requirement of under 5 seconds in quiet surroundings.
  • Hit rate of 90 percent in a quiet room, and 40 to 70 percent with background noise. Common misinterpretations were added as alternative commands.
  • The whole system runs locally, with no cloud services.
